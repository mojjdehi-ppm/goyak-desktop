# Baku Traffic Congestion: Scale, Causes, and Existing Government Measures (2024–2026)

Research date: 1 November 2026. Figures are the most recent found; older figures are flagged with their year. Azerbaijani institution names are given with originals where useful: AYNA = Azərbaycan Yerüstü Nəqliyyat Agentliyi (Azerbaijan Land Transport Agency, successor to Baku Transport Agency / BNA); NİİM = Nəqliyyatı İntellektual İdarəetmə Mərkəzi (Intelligent Transport Management Center, under the Ministry of Internal Affairs / DİN); AAYDA = Azərbaycan Avtomobil Yolları Dövlət Agentliyi (State Agency of Azerbaijan Automobile Roads); DSK = Dövlət Statistika Komitəsi (State Statistics Committee); OMM = Operativ Məlumat Mərkəzi (operativmm.az, Interior Ministry-linked news feed).

## Key Question 1: Quantitative indicators — how bad is Baku's congestion?

### Takeaway
No independent congestion index (TomTom/INRIX) publishes a Baku figure that I could find; the best quantitative anchors are official statements that ~630,000 people in ~300,000 cars flow toward central Baku daily, a national fleet of ~1.5 million cars (2023) / ~1.83 million registered vehicles (Dec 2024), ~550,000–750,000 vehicles in Baku depending on source, and Numbeo crowd-sourced data showing a 37.9-minute average one-way commute. The President himself said in January 2025 that jams are "becoming more frequent and severe each year."

### Cited Findings
- President Ilham Aliyev, 28 Jan 2025 transport meeting: traffic congestion in Baku remains persistent, "jams are becoming more frequent and severe each year"; national population grew from ~7 million to >10 million in 30 years and road/metro infrastructure has struggled to keep pace — [president.az speech, 28 Jan 2025](https://president.az/en/articles/view/68151/print)
- Anar Rzayev (Chairman, AYNA), 28 Jan 2025: more than 630,000 passengers travel toward central Baku daily, using roughly 300,000 cars; the Sumgayit–Khirdalan direction accounts for a large share of these flows — [APA, 28 Jan 2025](https://en.apa.az/infrastructure/plan-to-prevent-traffic-jams-in-center-of-baku-revealed-459213)
- Vehicles in Baku: IQAir (cited by Eurasianet, Feb 2026) puts registered vehicles in Baku at "just under 550,000" — [Eurasianet, 20 Feb 2026](https://eurasianet.org/azerbaijan-to-build-baku-tram-system); a strategic-planning official, Aqil Əsədov, cited "about 750,000 vehicles in Baku" (undated in search results) — [report.az](https://report.az/infrastruktur/baki-yollarinda-tixacin-sebebleri-arasdirilir). The two figures conflict; neither is an official DSK Baku registration count.
- National fleet: 1,501,957 passenger cars in 2023 (DSK via CEIC); 1,833,874 total registered vehicles in Dec 2024 (all categories) — [CEIC](https://ceicdata.com/en/azerbaijan/number-of-motor-vehicles); [CEIC registered vehicles](https://www.ceicdata.com.cn/en/indicator/azerbaijan/number-of-registered-vehicles)
- Motorisation: 148 passenger cars per 1,000 people in 2023, up from 127 in 2020 (ABB bank overview citing DSK) — [ABB automotive overview PDF](https://abb-bank.az/storage/uploads/files/1725443491_automotive-industry-overview-global-trends-and-azerbaijan.pdf)
- Car inflow 2025: 113,840 passenger cars, 8,163 trucks and 877 buses imported in 2025 — [Modern.az](https://modern.az/en/aktual/571197/over-113-thousand-cars-have-been-imported-into-azerbaijan); 4,038 passenger cars manufactured domestically in 2025 (DSK via Report.az) — [Report.az](https://report.az/en/amp/industry/azerbaijan-shares-data-on-auto-manufacturing-in-2025); Jan–Apr 2025 vehicle imports +21.3% y/y (State Customs Committee) — [Report.az](https://report.az/en/business/azerbaijan-sees-over-21-increase-in-auto-imports)
- Electric cars nationally: 5,941 electric passenger vehicles in 2024 vs 3,115 in 2023 (DSK) — [APA](https://en.apa.az/infrastructure/state-statistical-committee-reveals-number-of-electric-vehicles-in-azerbaijan-460206)
- Numbeo (crowd-sourced, 63 contributors, updated 4 Jan 2026): Baku Traffic Index 146.05; Time Index (avg one-way commute) 37.89 min; Inefficiency Index 179.47; CO2 Index 3,832.48; main commute mode: car 34.9%, bus 23.8%, walking 22.2%, metro 15.9%, bicycle 1.6% — [Numbeo Baku traffic](https://www.numbeo.com/traffic/in/Baku)
- Eurasianet characterises Baku as having "some of the worst traffic congestion of any major city in Eurasia" (journalistic assessment, no index cited) — [Eurasianet, 20 Feb 2026](https://eurasianet.org/azerbaijan-to-build-baku-tram-system)
- Peak-hour hotspot lists: NİİM publishes near-daily congestion bulletins listing 14–21 congested "directions"; e.g. 26 Feb 2025 (21 directions), 25 Sep 2025 (19 directions), 6 Jan 2026 (14 directions) — [Report.az 21 directions](https://report.az/infrastruktur/bakida-21-istiqametde-tixac-var-siyahi); [Report.az 19 directions](https://report.az/infrastruktur/bakinin-bir-sira-yollarinda-tixac-yaranib628); [Operativmm 14 roads](https://operativmm.az/en/post/bakida-14-yolda-tixac-hereket-cetinlesdi/71591)
- Most accident-prone corridors (NİİM camera data, 2025): Ziya Bunyadov, Heydar Aliyev, Babek, 8 November and Tbilisi avenues, plus Baku–Sumgayit, Airport and Sabunchu–Zabrat highways — [APA, 28 Jan 2026](https://en.apa.az/incident/over-2500-traffic-accidents-recorded-on-baku-roads-490131)
- Officially identified bottleneck: the Binagadi highway / Azadlig Avenue / "20 January" / Ziya Bunyadov intersections "frequently experience severe bottlenecks" (motivation for the planned Mehdiabad–Baksol–Ziya Bunyadov road) — [Operativmm](https://operativmm.az/en/post/mehdiabadbaksol-yolunda-1-yeni-magistral-insa-edilecek/73025)
- Breakdown-induced jams: 1,889 technically faulty vehicles broke down on Baku roads in 2025, causing "long-term congestion" (Main State Traffic Police Dept., Feb 2026) — [Operativmm, 12 Feb 2026](https://operativmm.az/en/post/bakida-tixaclarin-esas-sebebi-aciqlandi/67198)
- Public transport volumes as a denominator: 476 million passengers transported in Baku in 2024 (+28m vs 2023); buses on dedicated lanes carry ~900,000 passengers/day; Baku Metro 226.8 million passengers in 2025 (−1.4% vs 2024; 2024 avg 627,279/day) — [Mincom press conference, 10 Jan 2025](https://mincom.gov.az/en/media-en/news/press-conference-held-on-work-done-in-transport-sector-in-2024-and-projects-to-be-implemented-in-2025); [ABC.az metro 2025](https://abc.az/en/news/194234/last-year-the-number-of-passengers-on-baku-metro-decreased)
- Rainfall sharply worsens jams; drainage problems and large puddles cited by Baku traffic police — [Operativmm rain](https://operativmm.az/en/post/rain-causes-heavy-traffic-congestion-in-baku/69789); [JAM News (AZ)](https://jam-news.net/az/?p=6786)

### Inferences
- With ~300,000 cars carrying ~630,000 people inbound daily (≈2.1 persons/car as stated, though likely many single-occupant trips), the AYNA figures imply car commuting into the centre roughly matches the entire daily metro ridership (~620k) — the centre is heavily car-dependent despite a dense metro/bus network.
- The ~550k (IQAir) vs ~750k (official) Baku vehicle counts probably reflect registration-address vs vehicles-actually-circulating (including Absheron/Sumgayit commuters); a hackathon team should quote a range.
- Metro ridership flat/declining in 2025 while car imports exceed 110k/year suggests the modal shift the government wants is not yet happening.

### Gaps
- No TomTom Traffic Index or INRIX entry for Baku was found; Baku appears not to be among TomTom's 500 ranked cities (unverified — the ranking page is dynamic and could not be parsed). No official average commute time exists; Numbeo's 37.9 min is crowd-sourced with only 63 respondents.
- No official DSK figure for cars registered in Baku city for 2024/2025 was found; only national totals.
- No official per-corridor speed/delay statistics were found for Heydar Aliyev, Babek, Nobel, Koroglu, Ahmadli or Yasamal; NİİM bulletins list locations but not delay metrics.

## Key Question 2: Root causes cited by officials and experts

### Takeaway
Officials attribute congestion to population and car-ownership growth outpacing infrastructure, a mono-centric city with huge inbound suburban flows (especially Sumgayit–Khirdalan), peak-hour synchronisation of work/school start times, driver behaviour (lane discipline, breakdowns), fixed-time signals, and insufficient public transport attractiveness; independent experts add that enforcement and pricing, not just construction, are needed.

### Cited Findings
- Population and car growth vs infrastructure: President Aliyev (Jan 2025) — population grew from ~7m to >10m; roads and metro "struggled to keep pace" — [president.az](https://president.az/en/articles/view/68151/print); the Centre for Analysis of Economic Reforms (CAERC/ereforms) cites urbanisation, economic growth and rising access to private vehicles — [ereforms.gov.az, 6 Jan 2026](https://ereforms.gov.az/en/ekspert-yazilari/baki-ve-etraf-erazilerde-neqliyyat-infrastrukturunun-tekmillesdirilmesi-qlobal-cagirislar-ve-dovlet-proqraminin-rolu-149)
- Mono-centric commuting: 630k people / 300k cars inbound daily; Sumgayit–Khirdalan is the dominant corridor (AYNA) — [APA](https://en.apa.az/infrastructure/plan-to-prevent-traffic-jams-in-center-of-baku-revealed-459213). The Baku Master Plan 2040 explicitly aims to convert Baku into a "polycentric city" with a primary centre, regional centres and sub-centres — [arxkom.gov.az](https://arxkom.gov.az/en/media/xeberler/baki-seherinin-2040-ci-ile-qeder-olan-bas-plani-tesdiq-edildi)
- Peak-hour synchronisation: the 2025–2030 State Program proposes flexible/staggered working hours for state bodies, educational and medical institutions and private firms, plus peak/off-peak differentiated public transport fares — [Turan, 6 Feb 2025](https://turan.az/en/social/economist-on-traffic-congestion-the-government-should-not-use-this-as-an-excuse-to-raise-prices-790153); MP Vugar Bayramov (June 2026): "congestion cannot be solved by expanding infrastructure alone" — [1news.az, 11 Jun 2026](https://1news.az/en/news/20260611105509427-Vugar-Bayramov-Baku-may-soon-switch-to-flexible-work-schedule)
- School runs: NİİM applied "green wave" more frequently near school zones because density rises at the start of the school year (2015 statement, old) — [Minval, 24 Aug 2015](https://minval.az/news/123480406); Eurasianet reported police complaints about "lengthy family motorcades" at school start (older item) — [Eurasianet](https://eurasianet.org/azerbaijan-kids-start-school-with-lengthy-family-motorcades-cops-complain)
- Driver behaviour: official Aqil Əsədov argued the problem is not car numbers but drivers not keeping lane discipline — [Report.az](https://report.az/infrastruktur/baki-yollarinda-tixacin-sebebleri-arasdirilir); Traffic Police named breakdowns of technically faulty vehicles (1,889 in 2025) as a main cause of long-lasting jams — [Operativmm, 12 Feb 2026](https://operativmm.az/en/post/bakida-tixaclarin-esas-sebebi-aciqlandi/67198)
- Signal timing: NİİM says traditional fixed-schedule signals do not adapt to conditions — the stated rationale for the Dec 2025 adaptive-signal pilot — [1news.az, 18 Dec 2025](https://1news.az/az/news/20251218100458812-Bakida-ilk-defe-agilli-svetoforlar-qurashdirilib-Tixacin-qarshisi-alinacaq-VIDEO); an older (2017) Baku Transport Agency note blamed same-phase signals at a junction and a missing pedestrian crossing on Təbriz street for peak jams — [Report.az](https://report.az/infrastruktur/baki-yollarinda-tixacin-sebebleri-arasdirilir)
- Secondhand/high-emission car imports: IQAir notes many imported cars no longer meet emission standards in their origin country; Baku ranked 89th most polluted metro area on 19 Feb 2026 — [Eurasianet](https://eurasianet.org/azerbaijan-to-build-baku-tram-system). Aliyev: vehicles, particularly large ones, are among the leading air-pollution contributors — [president.az](https://president.az/en/articles/view/68151/print)
- Falling bus load factors despite more buses: average passengers per bus per day fell from 630 (2023) to 620 (2024) to 560 (2025) — [Report.az, Rzayev](https://report.az/en/infrastructure/anar-rzayev-nearly-400-green-buses-to-be-launched-in-baku-by-end-of-2025)
- Pricing/enforcement argument (older, 2018): Azernews/Trend op-ed argued demand outpaces road supply and proposed a central-Baku entry charge — [Azernews, 12 Jul 2018](https://www.azernews.az/nation/134690.html)
- Taxi oversupply addressed: taxi orders fell ~31–32% Dec 2024 vs Dec 2023 after reforms; ~35,000 taxi drivers trained — [Mincom, 10 Jan 2025](https://mincom.gov.az/en/media-en/news/press-conference-held-on-work-done-in-transport-sector-in-2024-and-projects-to-be-implemented-in-2025)

### Inferences
- The official diagnosis has shifted since 2011 (when Aliyev said infrastructure building "stopped traffic jams becoming a major problem") to a 2025–26 recognition that demand management (staggered hours, paid zones, peak fares) is required.
- Falling per-bus loads while total ridership rises suggest frequency is being added faster than demand — bus speed/reliability (not capacity) is the binding constraint, a gap AI scheduling or signal priority could address.

### Gaps
- No official modal-split survey for Baku trips (share by car/bus/metro/walk) was found; only Numbeo's small sample and the national passenger-share (88.3% road, 11.1% metro, Jan–Apr 2025) — [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4044153.html).
- No quantified attribution of congestion to school runs, construction sites or parking search was found for 2024–2026.

## Key Question 3: Existing Intelligent Transport System (ITS) in Baku

### Takeaway
Baku has had a Korean-built Intelligent Transport Management Center (NİİM, now under the Interior Ministry) since December 2011 with citywide camera coverage and centrally controlled signals; it runs "green wave" coordination (since 2015), publishes daily congestion bulletins and accident statistics from camera data, and in December 2025 launched its first adaptive ("smart") signal pilot at two Binagadi intersections with foreign specialists — still in trial in 2026, with 5–10 km/h speed gains claimed.

### Cited Findings
- Origin: the Intelligent Traffic Management Center opened 29 Dec 2011, modelled on Seoul's centre and built with Korean companies; Aliyev said only eight such centres existed worldwide — [president.az, 29 Dec 2011](https://president.az/en/articles/view/4076); first phase used 450 km of independent electrical lines and 320 km of fibre-optic lines (2011 figures) — [president.az (AZ)](https://president.az/az/articles/view/3223/print)
- Governance: NİİM is now the "Transport Intelligent Management Center of the Ministry of Internal Affairs" (Nəqliyyatı İntellektual İdarəetmə Mərkəzi, DİN); press secretary Ömər Xıdırov — [1news.az, 19 Mar 2026](https://1news.az/az/news/20260319015254246-NIIM-resmisi-Bakida-agilli-ishiqforlar-in-tetbiq-oldugu-yerlerde-neqliyyatin-sureti-artib-ACHIQLAMA). (In 2015 it sat under the Ministry of Transport — [Minval 2015](https://minval.az/news/123480406).) AYNA also installs signals under its own projects (e.g. Həsən Əliyev St / Tbilisi Ave, May 2026) — [1news.az, 14 May 2026](https://1news.az/az/news/20260514040457584-Bakinin-bu-erazilerinde-yeni-svetoforlar-istifadeye-verildi)
- Camera coverage: NİİM recorded 2,546 road accidents in 2025 "within the coverage area of video surveillance cameras on the capital's roads" — [APA, 28 Jan 2026](https://en.apa.az/incident/over-2500-traffic-accidents-recorded-on-baku-roads-490131); cameras detect signal faults and dispatch technicians; red-light running is fined 100 AZN + 3 penalty points (Art. 327.2) — [AZƏRTAC](https://azertag.az/xeber/neqliyyati_intellektual_idareetme_merkezi_vetendaslara_muraciet_edib-2971853)
- Green wave: signals on main streets/avenues began operating on a "green wave" principle in Aug 2015, prioritised near schools, with expansion planned once intensity statistics were collected — [Minval, 24 Aug 2015](https://minval.az/news/123480406)
- Adaptive-signal pilot (Dec 2025): first "smart traffic lights" at (a) Azadlıq Ave × Cəfər Xəndan × Məsud Davudoğlu streets in front of School No. 115 (pedestrian signal) and (b) Cəfər Xəndan St × Əhməd Rəcəbli, Anar Şükürov, İbrahimpaşa Dadaşov, Şövkət Məmmədova and Əlişir Nəvai streets, Binəqədi district; sensors, cameras and sometimes radars measure intensity, central software adjusts green durations in real time, re-prioritises after accidents/closures; launched with specialists from two (unnamed) foreign countries; expected 15–30% congestion reduction based on foreign experience — [1news.az, 18 Dec 2025](https://1news.az/az/news/20251218100458812-Bakida-ilk-defe-agilli-svetoforlar-qurashdirilib-Tixacin-qarshisi-alinacaq-VIDEO); [Mia.az](https://mia.az/w1093665/bakida-ilk-defe-ldquoagilli-svetoforlarrdquo-qurasdirilib-video-2025-12-18-110724)
- Pilot results (Mar 2026): speeds at the pilot intersections rose by 5–10 km/h; still in trial; no problems detected; expansion to other streets depends on results — [1news.az, 19 Mar 2026](https://1news.az/az/news/20260319015254246-NIIM-resmisi-Bakida-agilli-ishiqforlar-in-tetbiq-oldugu-yerlerde-neqliyyatin-sureti-artib-ACHIQLAMA)
- Signal time-plan changes are made manually on some roads (e.g. Pravda.az report of timing changes) — [Pravda.az](http://pravda.az/news/175153); new signals and re-organised flow at Yəhya Hüseynov × Rəşid İsmayılov streets, Binəqədi (Dec 2025) — [Report.az](https://report.az/daxili-siyaset/bineqedide-yol-hereketinde-deyisiklik-edilib-yeni-svetoforlar-qurasdirilib)
- Digital twin: AYNA built a digital twin of Baku and simulated >20 projects (2023) and 50 road projects (2025), selecting 9 priorities — [Mincom, 18 Dec 2023](https://mincom.gov.az/en/media-en/news/presentation-of-mobility-transformation-program-in-azerbaijan-took-place); [APA, 28 Jan 2025](https://en.apa.az/infrastructure/plan-to-prevent-traffic-jams-in-center-of-baku-revealed-459213)
- AYNA digital stack: BakuKart, Azparking and Biletim.az apps plus "AYNA Information System" subsystems; cashless fare share 85%, 1,800 buses with cashless payment (2026) — [Mincom 2023](https://mincom.gov.az/en/media-en/news/presentation-of-mobility-transformation-program-in-azerbaijan-took-place); [Modern.az, 28 Jan 2026](https://modern.az/en/aktual/562249/there-is-an-increase-of-over-67-million-in-baku-and-suburban-passenger-transportation)
- ITS is a named priority ("digitalization, Intelligent Transport Systems and traffic flow optimization") in the 2025–2030 State Program, but with no published quantitative targets — [ereforms.gov.az](https://ereforms.gov.az/en/ekspert-yazilari/baki-ve-etraf-erazilerde-neqliyyat-infrastrukturunun-tekmillesdirilmesi-qlobal-cagirislar-ve-dovlet-proqraminin-rolu-149)
- Baku Metro: all passenger carriages equipped with surveillance cameras (older item) — [APA](https://en.apa.az/infrastructure/-104536)

### Inferences
- Adaptive control covers only two intersections out of the whole city as of mid-2026; the vast majority of Baku's signals remain fixed-time or pre-set "green wave" plans — the biggest unsolved ITS gap and the most natural hackathon target (e.g. camera-based volume estimation to feed signal plans, or bus signal priority).
- NİİM already has the two ingredients an AI pilot needs — citywide camera feeds and central signal control — but the data is not public; a team would have to simulate or use proxy data.
- The split of responsibilities (NİİM/Interior Ministry runs signals and cameras; AYNA runs buses, parking and some signal installations; AAYDA builds roads) means bus-priority or integrated solutions need cross-agency data sharing.

### Gaps
- No current count of signalised intersections or cameras in Baku was found (only 2011 cabling figures). I found no evidence of a publicly described AI/computer-vision pilot beyond the adaptive-signal trial, and the foreign partner countries/vendor for the pilot are not named.
- No published evaluation methodology or before/after dataset for the adaptive pilot.

## Key Question 4: Recent policies 2024–2026 (roads, interchanges, bus lanes, parking, paid zones, metro, tram, car imports, electric buses, Master Plan 2040)

### Takeaway
The anchor policy is the State Program on Improving Transport Infrastructure in Baku and Surrounding Areas 2025–2030 (decree Jan 2025) built on the Baku Master Plan 2040 (approved 30 Dec 2023): 26 major road projects (29-km 6-lane bypass with 9 interchanges, Koroglu tunnels), bus lanes expanding from 114 km to 160 km, 10 new metro stations, a 5-line tram network tendered by end-2026, four-zone paid parking (17,000+ spaces), paid traffic (congestion-charge) zones in Baku from 1 Jan 2027, flexible working hours from 2026, and a fleet shift to ~400 electric/CNG buses — while hybrid/EV import tax breaks expired on 1 Jan 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Framework**
- Baku Master Plan 2040 approved 30 Dec 2023 (Cabinet Resolution No. 504), prepared by the State Urban Planning and Architecture Committee with AS+P Albert Speer + Partner, EY and Ramboll UK; polycentric structure; proposes metro and rail expansion, interchange hubs, cycling — [arxkom.gov.az](https://arxkom.gov.az/en/media/xeberler/baki-seherinin-2040-ci-ile-qeder-olan-bas-plani-tesdiq-edildi)
- State Program 2025–2030 approved by presidential decree in January 2025; monitored by CAERC; contents: 10 new metro stations, bus lanes to 160 km, tram and BRT in high-demand corridors, ring/radial roads, multi-level interchanges, tunnel-type overpasses, bicycle lanes, parking, pedestrian zones, ITS — [ereforms.gov.az, 6 Jan 2026](https://ereforms.gov.az/en/ekspert-yazilari/baki-ve-etraf-erazilerde-neqliyyat-infrastrukturunun-tekmillesdirilmesi-qlobal-cagirislar-ve-dovlet-proqraminin-rolu-149); [AIIB Green Line NTS 2026](https://www.aiib.org/en/projects/details/2026/_download/Azerbaijan/06_BMEP_Green-Line_NTS_EN.pdf)
- AZCON Holding created to coordinate transport (Aliyev, Jan 2025) — [president.az](https://president.az/en/articles/view/68151/print)

**Roads and interchanges**
- AAYDA is implementing 26 major road projects under the Program (Fariz Azizov, WUF13, May 2026): Bilajari–Khirdalan–Shamakhi highway (29 km, 6 lanes, 9 interchanges, 13 bridges, tunnels) linking M1 and M4 so regional traffic bypasses the centre; Academician Hasan Aliyev St–Koroglu metro connection (~50% complete, 4 tunnels + underground pedestrian crossing) to relieve Heydar Aliyev and Ziya Bunyadov avenues; Chanlibel tunnels; Hasan bey Zardabi–Hajiaga Rustamov link — [APA, 21 May 2026](https://en.apa.az/wuf13/measures-to-optimize-traffic-flow-in-baku-announced-507651)
- AYNA's integrated plan (Jan 2025): of 50 proposed road projects, 9 prioritised via digital twin; three ring roads (Bilgah–Novkhani–Khirdalan–Shamakhi; Sabunchu–Sulutepe; a link ring to Ziya Bunyadov Ave) plus six in-city bottleneck projects — [APA, 28 Jan 2025](https://en.apa.az/infrastructure/plan-to-prevent-traffic-jams-in-center-of-baku-revealed-459213)
- Pirshagi–Novkhani–Khirdalan–M4 highway ~51% complete (Sep 2025); new tunnel-type overpass started Nov 2025 — [Trend](https://www.trend.az/azerbaijan/society/4120453.html); Khirdalan: three tunnels under the railway to M4, new Khirdalan–M1–Masazir–Novkhani link, improved Khirdalan roundabout (Mar 2026) — [Trend](https://www.trend.az/azerbaijan/society/4151845.html)
- Mehdiabad–Baksol road to connect to 8 Noyabr Ave / Ziya Bunyadov to relieve Binagadi–20 Yanvar bottleneck — [Operativmm](https://operativmm.az/en/post/mehdiabad-baksol-road-to-be-connected-with-8-noyabr-avenue/73704)
- New 6-lane highway parallel to Ziya Bunyadov and Heydar Aliyev avenues (Hasan Aliyev St → Muzaffar Narimanov St) under construction Apr 2025 — [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4189232.html)
- Six traffic re-organisation projects completed in 2024 on Zarifa Aliyeva, Khagani, 28 May, Mikail Mushfig, Matbuat, Khojaly and Azadlig; three new transport hubs (Ganjlik, 28 May, Koroghlu south exit) — [Mincom, 10 Jan 2025](https://mincom.gov.az/en/media-en/news/press-conference-held-on-work-done-in-transport-sector-in-2024-and-projects-to-be-implemented-in-2025)
- National 20-year totals: 20.8k km roads (2.6k km in Baku), 335 bridges/overpasses, 45 tunnels, 163 pedestrian under/overpasses — [ereforms.gov.az](https://ereforms.gov.az/en/ekspert-yazilari/baki-ve-etraf-erazilerde-neqliyyat-infrastrukturunun-tekmillesdirilmesi-qlobal-cagirislar-ve-dovlet-proqraminin-rolu-149)

**Bus lanes and buses (AYNA)**

## Key Question 5: Pedestrian safety and accessibility

### Takeaway
Pedestrians are the dominant casualty group: 39.9% of all Azerbaijani road accidents in 2025 involved pedestrians, and in Baku 21 pedestrians were killed in Q1 2026 alone (43 of 68 accidents involved pedestrians). Baku has long favoured grade-separated crossings (163 under/overpasses built nationally over 20 years), which residents have criticised as inconvenient and which depend on escalators/lifts now being replaced after reaching end of life.

### Cited Findings
- National 2025 (DSK, 16 Feb 2026): accidents down 1,252 (−12.3%) vs 2024; 1,715 victims (−12.1%): 689 deaths, 1,026 injured; 85.1% of accidents in settlements; 39.9% of accidents involved pedestrians; 73.3% of deaths were men; 44.5% daytime, 39.4% night — [stat.gov.az](https://stat.gov.az/special_version/news/index.php?id=6619)
- National H1 2024: 333 deaths, 598 injured; 86.5% of accidents in residential areas — [APA](https://en.apa.az/incident/333-people-died-and-598-injured-in-road-accidents-in-first-half-of-2024-in-azerbaijan-445022)
- Baku Q1 2026 (NİİM data via OMM): 68 accidents, 43 involving pedestrians struck by vehicles, 21 pedestrians killed, 24 injured — [Operativmm, 27 Apr 2026](https://operativmm.az/en/post/21-pedestrians-killed-in-baku-traffic-accidents-in-q1/71044)
- Baku 2025 (NİİM camera coverage): 2,546 accidents; causes: speeding, red-light running, unsafe distance, sign violations, inattention — [APA, 28 Jan 2026](https://en.apa.az/incident/over-2500-traffic-accidents-recorded-on-baku-roads-490131)
- Older baselines: 107 pedestrians killed in Baku in 2019; in 2018 over half of 619 Baku accidents were pedestrian accidents — [JAM News](https://jam-news.net/107-people-died-under-the-wheels-of-cars-in-2019-in-baku/); nationally 821 road deaths in 2019 (World Bank) — [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/azerbaijan/publication/socio-economic-costs-and-human-impacts-of-road-accidents-in-azerbaijan)
- Long-term trend: 2024 accidents −46% and fatalities −32% vs 2014 (ITF bulletin via Trend) — [Trend](https://www.trend.az/azerbaijan/society/4133588.html)
- Grade-separated crossings: 163 underground/overground pedestrian crossings built nationally in 20 years — [ereforms.gov.az](https://ereforms.gov.az/en/ekspert-yazilari/baki-ve-etraf-erazilerde-neqliyyat-infrastrukturunun-tekmillesdirilmesi-qlobal-cagirislar-ve-dovlet-proqraminin-rolu-149); residents angered by a 10-metre-high overpass near the "Ukraine circle" (Neapol St, Khatai) replacing a zebra crossing, dubbed the "Great Baku Wall"; it opened June 2020 with 2 lifts for disabled users — [JAM News (AZ)](https://jam-news.net/az/boyuk-baki-səddi-10-metrlik-piyada-kecidi-azərbaycan-paytaxtinin-sakinlərini-qəzəbləndirib/); [president.az](https://president.az/az/articles/view/39261/print)
- Maintenance: escalators and some lifts at Baku pedestrian crossings that reached end of service life to be replaced (AZƏRTAC, 2 July, year unconfirmed) — [AZƏRTAC](https://azertag.az/xeber/bakida_piyada_kechidlerinde_istismar_muddetini_basa_vurmus_eskalator_ve_bezi_liftler_yenilenecek-4297139)
- Report.az investigation: not enough pedestrian crossings; people must walk long distances or cross dangerous roads — [Report.az](https://report.az/sosial-mudafie/bakida-piyada-zolaqlari-problemi)
- School-zone safety: Azerbaijan prioritises road safety around schools with FIA Foundation support — [FIA Foundation](https://fiafoundation.org/news/azerbaijan-prioritises-road-safety-around-schools-supported-by-fia-foundation); "Accessible Metro" project served ~4,000 passengers in 3 years — [Report.az](https://report.az/en/infrastructure/around-4-000-passengers-used-accessible-metro-project-in-baku-over-past-3-years)
- The State Program lists "pedestrian zones" expansion and "pedestrian access" as goals; AAYDA frames new tunnels as improving pedestrian safety — [APA, 21 May 2026](https://en.apa.az/wuf13/measures-to-optimize-traffic-flow-in-baku-announced-507651)

### Inferences
- If Q1 2026 is representative, Baku is on track for ~80+ pedestrian deaths in 2026 — close to 2019's 107, i.e. little improvement in the capital despite the national downward trend. (Own extrapolation; Q1 figure may be a camera-coverage subset.)
- The dominant engineering response (underpasses/overpasses) trades pedestrian convenience for vehicle throughput; surface signalised crossings with smart pedestrian detection (as in the School No. 115 pilot) are rare — an opening for AI-driven pedestrian-priority signals.

### Gaps
- No 2024 or 2025 full-year pedestrian death count for Baku specifically; no breakdown of deaths at marked vs unmarked crossings.
- No 2024–2026 sources found on organised complaints by elderly/disabled groups about underpasses; the only documented backlash is the 2020 Neapol St overpass.

## Key Question 6: Publicly available data sources for a demo

### Takeaway
There is no official open-data API from AYNA or NİİM; usable public inputs are limited to NİİM's text congestion bulletins (via report.az/operativmm.az), DSK statistical releases, commercial map traffic layers, an unverified Kaggle corridor dataset, and a dormant community GTFS effort — so a team should plan on synthetic or proxy data.

### Cited Findings
- NİİM congestion bulletins (lists of congested streets, several per day) are republished by Report.az and Operativmm.az in Azerbaijani/English/Russian — scrape-able text, not an API — [Report.az example](https://report.az/infrastruktur/bakida-21-istiqametde-tixac-var-siyahi); [Operativmm example](https://operativmm.az/en/post/current-traffic-situation-on-baku-roads-announced/72423)
- Kaggle dataset: an Azerbaijani data scientist (Turgut Abdullayev) states on LinkedIn that nearly 7 million traffic-jam records from "the country's most congested corridor" collected from Nov 2024 were published on Kaggle — [LinkedIn profile](https://az.linkedin.com/in/turgutabdullayev). I could not open the profile (HTTP 999) or locate the Kaggle listing to confirm title, schema or source (likely a maps API scrape).
- GTFS: a GitHub org "Transit for Baku" (BakuCity, tfb.az) hosts forks of GTFS tooling but no published Baku feed was found — [GitHub BakuCity](https://github.com/BakuCity)
- Official apps with (private) real-time data: BakuKart, Azparking, Biletim.az, AYNA Information System — [Mincom 2023](https://mincom.gov.az/en/media-en/news/presentation-of-mobility-transformation-program-in-azerbaijan-took-place); metro NFC usage >21 million passengers (2025) — [ABC.az](https://abc.az/public/en/news/190571/number-of-nfc-user-subway-passengers-exceeds-21-million)
- Statistics: DSK (stat.gov.az) publishes annual road-accident releases and transport tables — [stat.gov.az 2025 accidents](https://stat.gov.az/special_version/news/index.php?id=6619); Baku Metro publishes monthly ridership infographics — [metro.gov.az infographics](https://metro.gov.az/en/infographics/3499/2024-cu-ilin-ikinci-ayinda-sernisindasima)
- Numbeo crowd-sourced traffic/commute data for Baku (small sample) — [Numbeo](https://www.numbeo.com/traffic/in/Baku)
- Air quality proxy: IQAir daily city ranking for Baku — [Eurasianet citing IQAir](https://eurasianet.org/azerbaijan-to-build-baku-tram-system)
- World Bank "wazeasy" notebooks show how Waze partner data is used in traffic research (generic, not Baku-specific) — [World Bank wazeasy](https://worldbank.github.io/wazeasy/notebooks/example.html)

### Inferences
- Because AYNA's digital twin, NİİM camera feeds and adaptive-signal telemetry are closed, the most realistic demo pipeline is: (1) commercial map traffic tiles/routing APIs (Google/Yandex/TomTom cover Baku for live traffic colouring, though I did not verify terms), (2) scraped NİİM bulletins as ground-truth labels for congestion events, (3) DSK/NİİM accident statistics for safety weighting, (4) public camera/video footage or synthetic simulation (e.g. SUMO) of the named corridors.

### Gaps
- I did not verify whether Yandex Maps, Google Maps or Waze expose live traffic for Baku, nor their API licensing; I found no AYNA developer portal or Azerbaijani national open-data portal entry for transport.
- The Kaggle dataset could not be verified; treat as a lead, not a source.
