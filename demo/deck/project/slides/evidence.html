This is not a new, unproven idea. Washington’s WMATA cut excess waiting time by 26 percent. Chicago’s CTA cut waits by 6 to 20 percent during 2022 and 2023, even while 15 to 25 percent of trips were cancelled for lack of drivers, and with only about half of drivers following the advice. Singapore cut excess waiting time by 15 to 25 percent. Chicago is the closest to Baku’s situation: not enough buses and heavy traffic.
