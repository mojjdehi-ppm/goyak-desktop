Buses are Baku’s main public transport: about 494 million trips a year and around 2,500 buses daily. But buses on the same route often arrive together, two or three at a time, and then there is a long gap. In October BakuBus’s own monitoring centre measured 17-minute intervals on route 2 against a planned 7 to 8, and 22 minutes on route 7A against a planned 4 to 5.
