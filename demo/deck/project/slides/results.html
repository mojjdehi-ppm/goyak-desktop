11 · Simulated buses and passengers on the real route 61. The baseline reproduces the 15–22-minute gaps BakuBus measured on routes 2 and 7A in Oct 2026.

This is our own simulation, and we show all of it. Without control the line bunches completely. Today’s practice, releasing buses on time at the terminal only, still gives 21-minute waits, which matches the 17 to 22-minute gaps BakuBus measured in October. If every driver followed the advice with live GPS, waits would fall to 5 minutes; that row is an upper bound, and we do not claim it. The Chicago-like row is what we design for: half the drivers comply, GPS every 60 seconds, one bus not reporting. The pessimistic row shows what happens when only a few drivers comply: almost nothing. So our claim for a first pilot is 5 to 15 percent less excess waiting, the range real cities achieved.
