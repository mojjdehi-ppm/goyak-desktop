Data and privacy

What is real, what is ours, what is synthetic

Real

  • Route 61 stops and shape from AYNA’s public map
  • Streets, metro and places from OpenStreetMap
  • BakuBus boardings and metro entries from opendata.az

Ours

  • Bus positions read from the public AYNA app for the pilot route
  • Manual counts at a few stops to check the crowding index
  • A pilot would use the official feed under a data agreement

Synthetic, labelled

  • Timetable, speeds and passengers in the simulation
  • Reserve-bus count at terminals
  • Driver compliance, taken from Chicago’s field figures

No driver rankings

Aggregated passenger data only

Hosted in Azerbaijan

Operator is the data controller

13 · Law 998-IIIQ on personal data: consent, purpose limitation, in-country processing

Three kinds of data, clearly separated. Real: the route, the streets, the open ridership tables. Ours: positions we read from the public AYNA app and manual counts at stops; a pilot would use the official feed under a data agreement. Synthetic and labelled as such: the timetable, speeds, passengers and the reserve count in our simulation, with driver compliance taken from Chicago’s field figures. On privacy: no driver rankings, passenger data only in aggregate, hosting in Azerbaijan, and the operator stays the data controller.
