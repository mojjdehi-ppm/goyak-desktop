Even gaps, without rushing anyone

  • Terminal first: even departures, where most bunching is born
  • After everyone has boarded and got off, it suggests the earliest time to depart
  • Never skips a stop and never cuts boarding short
  • If an elderly passenger needs longer, the suggested wait drops to zero

This is the most important point for safety. The driver always serves every stop, and every passenger boards at their own pace. Only after boarding is finished, the tablet may show an earliest departure time, here 40 seconds, because the bus behind is further away than the bus ahead. It is never an order to leave and never an order to skip a stop. If boarding takes longer, for example for an elderly passenger, the suggested wait simply becomes zero. We start at the terminal, where most bunching is born, and add two or three control points at metro transfer stops. The existing screen shows timetable deviation; ours shows the gap, on a separate tablet, only while the bus is stationary.
