One small delay feeds itself until buses bunch

A fixed timetable cannot correct this on its own. Traffic and a shortage of buses make it worse.

03 · Known since Newell and Potts (1964); Daganzo (2009)

Bunching is a well-known instability. When one bus is late, more passengers wait at each stop, so boarding takes longer and it gets even later. The bus behind it finds fewer passengers, moves faster and catches up. A timetable alone cannot fix this; it needs active control based on the real gaps between buses.
