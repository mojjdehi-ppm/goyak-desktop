08 · Pool size and hubs (3–4) are our assumption, to be agreed with AYNA

AYNA can keep a small standby pool, about 20 buses and minibuses at three or four hubs. When the forecast sees a big gap or a crowd coming, the system picks which vehicle to send, from which hub and where it should join the route. It compares the drive from the hub with how many passenger minutes it saves. The dispatcher sees the reason and approves with one tap. The pool size is our assumption and would be agreed with AYNA.
