We propose a small, low-risk pilot: one route for eight weeks. In week one we measure today’s numbers from GPS and publish the first regularity figure for a Baku route. Then we switch Ritm on, terminal first, and compare. Our targets: excess waiting time 5 to 15 percent lower at real driver compliance, bunched buses under 5 percent as a target, and irregularity at 0.3 or lower. It needs no new hardware. BakuBus on route 2 or 7A is the natural first host, because that is where the gaps were measured and the dispatch loop is closed; Xaliq Faiqoğlu on route 61 is the second.
