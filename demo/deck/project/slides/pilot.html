We propose a small, low-risk pilot: one route for eight weeks. In week one we measure today’s numbers from GPS. Then we switch Ritm on and compare. Our targets: excess waiting time at least 15 percent lower, bunched buses under 5 percent, and gap irregularity at 0.3 or lower. It needs no new hardware. Route 49 is a good candidate because bunching is reported there, or one of the gross-cost routes where AYNA already tracks intervals.
