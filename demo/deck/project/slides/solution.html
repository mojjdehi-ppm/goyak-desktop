The solution

Ritm: three AI modules on the GPS AYNA already has

Headway Guardian

Spots bunching early and keeps the gaps between buses even

Crowding Index

Flags the stops most at risk before the next bus, from headways and place types

Add-a-bus Advisor

Ranks where a reserve bus the carrier already holds would help most

No new hardware and no cameras: existing GPS, plus a tablet beside the on-board unit on pilot buses.

06

Ritm is software that sits on top of the GPS data AYNA and the carriers already collect. It has three modules that share one data pipeline. Headway Guardian keeps gaps even; it is the core. The crowding index flags the stops most at risk. The add-a-bus advisor ranks where a reserve bus the carrier already holds would help most, and the dispatcher decides. We need no new hardware and no cameras: a tablet beside the existing on-board unit on the pilot buses.
