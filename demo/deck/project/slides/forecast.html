Module 2 · Crowd Forecast

Knowing where crowds will form, before they do

What is around each stop

Schools, universities, shopping centres, hospitals and metro exits within 500 m. A stop by a university peaks at 08:30 and 17:30.

Every bus is a traffic sensor

Where buses slow down is where the traffic is. About 2,500 buses already map congestion street by street.

LightGBM model · inputs: past passenger counts, metro entries, time, weather, events

07

The forecast combines two things. First, what is around each stop: schools, universities, shopping centres, hospitals, metro exits. Each kind of place has its own daily rhythm. Second, the buses themselves: comparing how long a bus takes on a street with the free-flow time gives a live congestion index, so we see traffic without any camera. The model is LightGBM, which is fast, accurate and explainable.
