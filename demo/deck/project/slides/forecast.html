Module 2 · Crowding Index

Knowing which stops will crowd, before the bus arrives

What is around each stop

Schools, universities, shopping centres, hospitals and metro exits within 500 m. A stop by a university peaks at 08:30 and 17:30.

Every bus is a traffic sensor

Where buses slow down is where the traffic is. About 2,500 buses already map congestion street by street.

An index, low / medium / high, from headways and place profiles. It becomes a forecast when AYNA shares stop-level tap counts; until then we quote no accuracy figure.

08

The crowding index combines two things. First, what is around each stop: schools, universities, clinics, metro exits. Each kind of place has its own daily rhythm. Second, the buses themselves: comparing how long a bus takes on a street with the free-flow time gives a live congestion index, so we see traffic without any camera. We call it an index, not a forecast, on purpose: without stop-level tap counts nobody can honestly quote an accuracy figure. When AYNA shares those counts, the same pipeline becomes a forecast.
