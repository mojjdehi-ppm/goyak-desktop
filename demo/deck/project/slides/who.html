Who it hurts

Uneven buses cost people time, comfort and safety

Students and workers

Wait too long, arrive late, then ride on an overcrowded bus

Elderly and disabled

Stand long at the stop, then board a crowded bus in a rush

The whole city

People give up on the bus and drive, so traffic gets worse

04

This is why the project fits a human-centred city. Bunching hits students and workers with long waits and crowded buses. It hits elderly and disabled passengers hardest: long standing, then rushed boarding. And every person who gives up on the bus and drives adds to Baku’s traffic.
