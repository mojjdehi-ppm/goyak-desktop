The timing is right. AYNA already has live bus positions in its app. It is building a data warehouse. Since September, gross-cost contracts on five routes attach financial penalties to interval performance, and dispatcher services and software are reimbursable cost items for carriers. In October AYNA tendered an AI diagnostics platform: it analyses occupancy, demand and slow zones for analysts; it does not advise a driver or place a bus. Ritm is the operations module that sits on top of whatever platform AYNA buys.
